A little about me

I'm a Bachelor of Information and Communication Technology (BICT) undergraduate at the University of Vavuniya, currently building my foundation across multiple programming languages — Java, Python, C, C++ and C# — while exploring web development, data analysis and computer vision through self-directed projects.

What I enjoy most is turning an everyday, observable problem — long hospital waiting times, sorting cricket data, controlling a game without touching anything — into a small working prototype. I'm still early in my journey and genuinely curious about the parts of software engineering I haven't touched yet, which is why I keep a steady habit of reading up on topics I don't fully know.

Career objective: to secure an internship or entry-level developer role where I can apply what I've learned, work alongside experienced engineers, and grow into a dependable, problem-solving software developer.
